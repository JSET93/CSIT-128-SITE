About Travel Recommendation

Learn who we are, what we stand for, and how our team works together.

Company Information

Travel Recommendation was founded to make trip planning simple and enjoyable. We curate destinations across the globe — from historic cities to hidden beaches — and present them in one easy-to-browse place. Our small team researches each location personally, so every recommendation on this site is one we would genuinely suggest to a friend.

Our mission is to connect travelers with places that match their interests, whether that means culture, adventure, relaxation, or cuisine. We believe good travel advice should be honest, current, and easy to search — which is exactly what this website is built to do.

Guidance for Team Members

The following guidelines apply to anyone contributing content or code to this site:

  • Keep destination descriptions factual, concise, and free of exaggerated claims.
  • Use only images with permitted usage rights, and credit sources where required.
  • Test the search bar and Clear button after any change to the Home page cards.
  • Keep the navigation bar identical across all three pages (Home, About Us, Contact Us).
  • Write clear, semantic HTML and comment any non-obvious JavaScript logic.
  • Submit changes through pull requests so they can be reviewed before going live.
